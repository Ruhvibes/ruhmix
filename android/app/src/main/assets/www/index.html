Storage

🔒 Privacy

• Your audio stays on your device. Editing, FX, mixing — everything happens on-device, nothing is uploaded.

• The cloud is only used for AI Stem Separation — and only with your explicit consent. No audio is sent to the cloud without consent. The Modal option runs after watching an ad; the Hugging Face option is completely free (no ads).

• Instant auto-delete: audio sent for AI stems is removed from the server as soon as processing finishes — no copy is saved.

• Ads: one rewarded video ad before Modal AI Stem Separation (this pays for the server), and one interstitial ad after every 2nd export (max 1 per 5 min). Your data never leaves the device because of ads.

• No account, no tracking — your files are yours.

Removes stem results, stem-mix preview, peaks cache and temporary data. Saved projects stay safe.
